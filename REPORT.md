## 4. Trade replay

No real trade history exists for this league, so trades are replayed inside the synthetic leagues: 4 random same-position 1-for-1 trades per snapshot. The model predicts team A's change in rest-of-regular-season starter points; we check whether team A really gained points (real lineups, real scores).

- Trades replayed: **719**
- Model picked the side that really gained points: **65.8%** (± 1.8% SE if the trades were independent; they share leagues and weeks, so the true uncertainty is somewhat larger; 50% = coin flip)
- When the model predicted a gap of at least 10 points: **73.1%** of 309 trades
- Correlation of predicted vs realized gain: 0.552

## What this does not show

- Real leagues have waivers, trades and managers who don't always start their best lineup; the synthetic leagues don't.
- FantasyCalc market values have no history, so the market check and acceptance heuristic are not backtested.
- ESPN K/DEF and the FantasyPros CSV are not part of the accuracy comparison.
- Archived Sleeper and ESPN projections are assumed to be the ones published before each week; if a source rewrote them later, its accuracy here would look better than it was.
- Synthetic leagues are drafted from noisy week-1 projections (no historical ADP), and 2021 and earlier aren't used.
- Standard errors and Brier scores treat team-snapshots and trades as independent, though they share leagues and weeks.
